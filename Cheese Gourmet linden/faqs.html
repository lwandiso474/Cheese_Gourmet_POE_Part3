Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our products, orders and platters. Select a question to open it.

What platters do you offer?

We offer a small platter (R250, serves 2-4 people), a medium platter (R450, serves 6-8 people) and a large grazing table (R850, serves 10-15 people). See the Platters & Catering page for what each one includes.

How do I order a platter?

Fill in the platter request form on the Contact page, WhatsApp us, or phone us on 011 888 5387. You can also add platters to your cart on the Products page to see your total first.

How much notice do you need?

We need 24 hours' notice for platter orders. If you need something bigger, such as a wedding or corporate event, contact us as early as you can.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver and set up in Johannesburg. Tell us your address and the date you need the platter when you send your request.

Where are you and when are you open?

We are at 71A 7th Street (corner of 3rd Avenue) in Linden, Johannesburg. We are open Monday to Friday 08:00-17:00 and Saturday 08:00-15:00, and closed on Sundays.

What cheeses and other products do you sell?

We stock a wide selection of locally produced cheeses, including cheddar, brie, gouda and blue cheese, as well as breads, preserves, crackers, olives and gourmet sauces.

Can I ask for something special?

Yes. Add your special requests in the message box on the request form, and we will get back to you to confirm what is possible.

Still have a question? Contact us and we will help.
