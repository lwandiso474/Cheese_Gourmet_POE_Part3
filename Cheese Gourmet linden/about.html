Meet Cheese Gourmet Linden

“From stealing extra slices of cheese as a child to studying the art behind this beautiful ingredient, what started as a craving quickly turned into a burning passion.”

Our story

After finishing culinary school and spending time in the industry, the idea of Cheese Gourmet started to form. I started my time learning valuable skills and working with unique flavours and pairings. It became a burning passion of mine.

Our mission

Our mission is to provide customers with quality gourmet products and excellent service that goes beyond taste. We are committed to using high quality ingredients, supporting local farmers, and delivering beautifully crafted cheese platters that bring people together, whether for everyday enjoyment or special celebrations.
